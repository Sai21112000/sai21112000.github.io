> "We shape our tools and thereafter our tools shape us."
{: .animate-enter style="animation-delay: 0.2s;"}

John Culkin. 1967. Observations on how the frameworks we choose constrain the way we solve problems in software engineering. What starts as a convenience slowly shapes our entire methodology.
{: .quote-note .animate-enter style="animation-delay: 0.3s;"}

> "Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication."
{: .animate-enter style="animation-delay: 0.4s;"}

Why stripping away JavaScript and focusing purely on typography and whitespace fundamentally improves the web. It is infinitely harder to design an elegant system with heavy constraints than to hide behind a generic UI library.
{: .quote-note .animate-enter style="animation-delay: 0.5s;"}
