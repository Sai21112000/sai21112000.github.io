I'm Sai — a computer vision and remote sensing engineer based in Thailand.

I recently completed my Master's degree in Information and Communications Technology at the [Asian Institute of Technology](https://www.ait.ac.th/). My thesis focused on **detecting and estimating canopy size of oil palm plantations** using YOLOv8, YOLOv11, and the Segment Anything Model (SAM) on UAV imagery. I worked across multiple ground sampling distances, built end-to-end segmentation pipelines, and compared instance segmentation approaches for precision agriculture.

## What I work with

- **Computer Vision** — YOLO family, SAM, Mask R-CNN, instance segmentation, object detection
- **Remote Sensing** — UAV imagery processing, multi-GSD analysis, canopy metrics, geospatial ML
- **Stack** — Python, PyTorch, Ultralytics, OpenCV, GDAL, QGIS, Roboflow, COCO/YOLO formats
- **Currently learning** — LLM agents, agentic systems, reinforcement learning

## Beyond the screen

I compete in powerlifting (IPF-registered) and believe that training the body is as important as training the mind. I'm also into photography, content creation, and making AI more accessible through education.

## What this blog is about

This is where I write about things I'm learning, building, and thinking about — computer vision, AI tools, web development, and the intersection of research and real-world engineering. No frameworks, no fluff. Just [pure HTML and CSS](/posts/first-post.html).
